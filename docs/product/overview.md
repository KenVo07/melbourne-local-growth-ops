# Product overview

## Status

Melbourne Local Growth Ops is one website-led product family. It has three
independently sellable capabilities:

1. Website & Lead Systems
2. Google Presence Operations
3. Reputation Operations

Website & Lead Systems is the primary engineering focus and preferred entry
offer. Google Presence Operations and Reputation Operations can be sold and
delivered without a website, including through manual-first workflows.

## Customer and problem

The initial customers are independent Melbourne contractors and restaurants
with weak or absent websites, fragmented lead or booking systems, unmanaged
Google Business Profiles, or inconsistent review follow-up. The initial buyer is
usually an owner-operator, director, practice or venue manager, or operations
manager.

The product addresses lost enquiries, duplicated administration, disconnected
business systems, slow complaint detection, inconsistent online information, and
poor attribution of calls, forms, and bookings.

## Product shape

- Capabilities can be purchased alone, in pairs, or as a package.
- Packages are commercial combinations, not runtime dependencies.
- Capability activation, onboarding, permissions, delivery, billing, pausing,
  cancellation, and data boundaries remain independent.
- Shared approved business identity may support onboarding, reporting, and
  cross-sell without coupling capability lifecycles.
- Website launch must not depend on Google Presence or Reputation automation.

See [Capability model](capability-model.md) for the canonical domain concepts.

## Current delivery focus

The early build sequence is:

1. Lock product, ownership, delivery, and integration contracts.
2. Build the private Website Factory and isolated deployment pipeline.
3. Build the contractor template and quote/lead path.
4. Launch a real contractor client.
5. Add the restaurant template and booking/order adapters.
6. Reconsider seller/catalog expansion.
7. Automate Reputation and Google Presence only after manual service validation.

## Scope boundaries

Initially in scope:

- Conversion-focused contractor and restaurant sites
- Quote, contact, booking, order-link, payment-link, and analytics integrations
- Managed-isolated and client-handoff delivery
- Standalone Google profile audits, approved changes, and reporting
- Standalone review drafting, approval, escalation, and complaint insights

Initially out of scope:

- Shared public multi-tenant website hosting
- Custom ecommerce backends or replacement POS/CRM systems
- Custom inventory, tax, checkout, refund, shipping, or fulfilment systems
- Autonomous sensitive review replies
- Guaranteed rankings or lead volumes
- Paid ads management
- Unlimited bespoke development
